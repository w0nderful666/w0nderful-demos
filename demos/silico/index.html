Abstract

We present SILICO, a standardized evaluation suite conducted on a single desk in a room with questionable ventilation. Our benchmark, known as ThermalBench, measures how quickly a processor can reach critical temperatures under trivial workloads. Results show that SILICO achieves thermal throttling 73% faster than competing solutions, establishing a new paradigm in "performance floor" computing.
